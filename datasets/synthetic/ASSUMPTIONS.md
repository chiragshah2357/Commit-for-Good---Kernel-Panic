# Assumptions for the synthetic data

Generated by `datasets/build_datasets.py`, random seed **42**. Where a value comes from a real source it is
marked *real*; where it was chosen by the team it is marked *guess*. Nothing here is a measurement.

## What is real and what is generated

| Item | Origin |
|---|---|
| Road geometry, road category, owner | real (PMGSY GeoSadak, July 2022 snapshot) |
| Habitation locations and population | real (PMGSY GeoSadak) |
| Health-facility locations and names | real (PMGSY GeoSadak "Medical" category, veterinary entries removed by name) |
| Rivers | real (OpenStreetMap) |
| Terrain heights | real (AWS terrain tiles) |
| Facility stock and demand | **generated** |
| Depot, fleet | **generated** |
| Flood scenarios | **generated** from real rivers and terrain |

## Parameters

| Parameter | Value | Basis |
|---|---|---|
| Facility catchment | each habitation served by its nearest facility (straight line) | guess; simple and transparent |
| Demand | 0.002 treatment courses per person per day | guess; abstract unit, not clinical data |
| Facility stock | demand x days of cover | guess |
| Days of cover | lognormal, median 14, sigma 0.5, clipped to 2-60 | guess; gives a mix of well and poorly stocked facilities |
| Depot stock | 10 days of district-wide demand | guess |
| Depot location | Silchar town centre | guess; not a real warehouse |
| Fleet | 8 trucks of 3,000 units; 2 boats of 800 units (boats unused by the road model) | guess |
| Road speed by category (km/h) | NH 50, SH 40, MDR 30, RR(ODR) 25, RR(VR) 20, BR 20, RR(TRACK) 10 | guess; no measured speeds |
| Flood severity (height above nearest river, max distance) | mild 1.0 m / 150 m, moderate 2.5 m / 600 m, severe 4.5 m / 1,500 m | tuned so about 8%, 24%, 44% of road length is cut; **not calibrated to any real event** |
| Replicates | 20 per severity, each with lognormal jitter (sigma 0.25) on height and distance | guess |
| Segment cut rule | segment midpoint is within the distance of a river and lower than the height above it | simplification of flood physics |
| Facility outage | a flooded facility is out of service with probability 0.5 | guess |

## What this data cannot show

- It does not show real stock levels, real consumption or real flood extents.
- Results on it demonstrate that a method works under stated assumptions; they are not findings about
  Cachar. The honest claim is "on a simulated flood with these assumptions, the method reduced unmet demand
  by X%".
- Flood severity is not tied to a historical event. A planned improvement is to replace the synthetic extent
  with an NRSC inundation map for a dated event, if a usable vector layer can be obtained.
- Rivers include small streams and canals mapped in OpenStreetMap, which makes the flood footprint blocky.
- Elevation comes from 30 m-class terrain data resampled to about 150 m, which is coarse on flat floodplains.

## Sensitivity

Evaluation should repeat results across severities and across the parameter ranges above, and report the range.
